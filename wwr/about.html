Whitewater Rafting Co.

Our Mission

Providing safe, exhilarating, and unforgettable white water rafting adventures for river enthusiasts of all experience levels.

History

Founded in 1998, Whitewater Rafting Co. began with a single raft and a passion for western rivers. Over the past two decades, we have guided thousands of adventure seekers down class III-V rapids with an unmatched safety record.

Our experienced guides are certified in wilderness first aid and river rescue, ensuring every excursion is both thrilling and secure.
